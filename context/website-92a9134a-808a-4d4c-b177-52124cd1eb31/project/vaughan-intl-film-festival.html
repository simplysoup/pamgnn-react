Vaughan Int'l Film Festival

Client:
Vaughan Int'l Film Festival
Category:
Motion Graphics, Social Media Graphics
No items found.

During my time working with VFF I worked with the team to redefine the 2024 style for the festival. Along with the Manager of Marketing and 2 other team members focused on graphic design we laid out the new style and created the full graphics for festival run. From countdowns, holiday posts, annoucements of films, presenters, winners, to email blast graphics, and more.

With my motion graphic, animation and video experience I was also tasked to worked along the video team to create two videos (with landscape and mobile versions). First up the VFF By the Numbers video which showcases the festival so far and how much it has grown. Secondly an informative video for submissions for the student flim competition. Highschool students across Ontario are open to submit their short films tobe screened infront of a live audience and evaluated by professionals in the film industry with the chance to win prizes for their team and school.
